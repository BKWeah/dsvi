[SCENE START]

**TITLE: Last Eid**

**LOGLINE:** A romantic conversation about the future takes an unexpected turn when a woman reveals to her Muslim boyfriend that she cannot marry him due to their religious differences, shattering his hopes for their life together.

**EXT. PARK - DAY**

Sunlight filters through the leaves of a large oak tree, dappling a lush green lawn.

AMIR (28), handsome with a warm and genuine smile, and CHLOE (27), bright-eyed with a thoughtful and direct nature, are nestled closely on a picnic blanket. An empty basket sits beside them. They are the picture of a happy couple.

Amir gently tucks a stray strand of hair behind Chloe's ear. His touch lingers on her cheek.

AMIR
 I was thinking...

Chloe smiles, leaning into his touch.

CHLOE
 (Playful)
 That's always a dangerous pastime for you.

He chuckles, his eyes full of adoration.

AMIR
 No, seriously. I was thinking about our kids.

Chloe’s smile tightens almost imperceptibly.

CHLOE
 Our kids?

AMIR
 Yeah. Do you think they'll have your eyes? I hope they have your eyes. But my charming wit, of course.

He winks. Chloe looks down, picking at a loose thread on the blanket. Her expression shifts.

CHLOE
 I haven’t really thought that far ahead.

AMIR
 (Lightly)
 Really? I think about it all the time. Us, a little house with a garden, a couple of loud, messy kids running around.

He watches her, expecting a reciprocal smile. He finds a quiet sadness instead.

AMIR
 (CONT'D)
 (Concerned)
 What's wrong?

CHLOE
 Amir... I haven't thought that far ahead with us because... I don't see us getting married.

The words hang in the air, thick and heavy. Amir’s smile evaporates, replaced by utter confusion.

AMIR
 What? What are you talking about?

CHLOE
 I've told you this before. I can't marry you. You know I'm a Christian, and my faith is important to me. I can only marry a Christian.

Amir pulls back, his voice a mixture of disbelief and hurt.

AMIR
 You mentioned it once, ages ago. I thought we were past that. I thought what we have was stronger than that.

CHLOE
 It's not about being "past it." It's who I am. It's what I believe.

AMIR
 So what is this, then? What have we been doing for the past two years?

CHLOE
 We're dating. We're enjoying each other's company. I love spending time with you, Amir. You know I do.

Amir lets out a sharp, humorless laugh.

AMIR
 (Scoffs, incredulous)
 Enjoying each other's company? Chloe, I'm in this for a future. For a life with you.

He scrambles to his feet, pacing in front of the blanket. The idyllic scene feels like a cruel joke.

AMIR
 (CONT'D)
 I've been in this relationship hoping to marry you. To build that life I just talked about. I thought that was the point.

Chloe remains on the blanket, her face a mask of pained resignation.

CHLOE
 I'm sorry if you thought that. I truly am. But I was always honest with you.

AMIR
 (Shakes his head, angry)
 No. You weren't. You let me believe. You let me hope.

He looks at her one last time, his heart and his future shattering in the same instant.

Without another word, he turns and walks away, a solitary figure disappearing across the sun-dappled lawn.

Chloe watches him go, left alone on the blanket.
